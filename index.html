Welcome to Rev Vivian Asamoah Ministry

Empowering Athletes • Faith • Excellence

We support people and communities with moral guidance, counseling, wellness support and sports development rooted in service and hope.

Who we are

Championing spiritual care and sports excellence.

Rev Vivian Asamoah Ministry is committed to nurturing the whole person through faith, mentorship, and sporting excellence. We walk alongside police personnel, athletes, students, and local communities, promoting resilience, purpose, and unity.

Our chaplaincy integrates pastoral care, community engagement, and sports outreach to inspire positive living, discipline, and service across Ghana.

What we do

Programs that transform lives and communities.

✝️

Spiritual Guidance

As a Pastor and Sports Chaplain, we are committed to nurturing the spiritual well-being of athletes, coaches, officers, and sports administrators. We provide Christ-centered spiritual guidance that strengthens faith, builds godly character, and equips individuals to face both personal and professional challenges with wisdom, courage, and integrity.

Through biblical counseling, prayer, mentorship, devotional sessions, and pastoral care, we help individuals develop spiritual resilience, maintain emotional balance, and discover their God-given purpose. Our mission is to inspire excellence on and off the field by fostering values such as discipline, teamwork, humility, perseverance, and servant leadership.

We believe that true success is achieved when physical ability is complemented by spiritual strength. Therefore, we are dedicated to walking alongside every athlete and officer, offering hope, encouragement, and practical biblical principles that empower them to excel in their careers while honoring God in every aspect of life.

🏅

Counseling Services

We provide professional, confidential, and compassionate counseling services tailored to the unique needs of athletes, officers, coaches, and their families. Our counseling approach integrates biblical principles, emotional support, and practical guidance to promote healing, personal growth, and overall well-being.

Whether facing personal struggles, career pressures, emotional challenges, relationship difficulties, or life transitions, we offer a safe and supportive environment where individuals can receive wise counsel, encouragement, and hope.

Our goal is to foster sustainable transformation by developing strong character, emotional resilience, spiritual maturity, and personal accountability. We empower officers and athletes to overcome challenges with wisdom, grace, integrity, and confidence, enabling them to excel both in their professional responsibilities and personal lives.

Through individual counseling, crisis intervention, family support, and follow-up care, we remain committed to restoring hope, strengthening lives, and helping every individual fulfill their God-given purpose.
